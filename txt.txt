Plan
- Build Arpège e-boutique (instruments) reusing AngularCRUD patterns (standalone comps, HttpClient, routing, json-server).
- No auth, no payment.
- Features: Home (landing), Catalog (list/add cart), Product detail, Cart (in-memory), CRUD-ready API service, model.
- Data source: json-server on db.json (instruments).
